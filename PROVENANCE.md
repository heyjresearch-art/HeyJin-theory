Copyright (c) 2026 J. San Park

## Purpose

HeyJin Theory is intended to be examined, criticized, reproduced, adapted, and extended within the permissions of the repository license. This document records a complementary research principle: when knowledge changes through reuse or transformation, the history of the structure should remain traceable.

> **Knowledge may change as it is shared, but the structure of how it developed should remain traceable.**

This repository therefore treats provenance not merely as a citation formality, but as part of the research structure itself.

---

## 1. Core Provenance Principle

A useful record of a derived or transformed structure should preserve three distinguishable elements:

**Origin → Transformation → New Structure**

- **Origin** — the identifiable source structure, document, paper, figure, or recorded research state from which the work developed.
- **Transformation** — what was changed, extended, criticized, recombined, reinterpreted, or independently verified.
- **New Structure** — the resulting contribution and the person, group, or system responsible for it.

The objective is not to prevent transformation. The objective is to prevent transformation from making the development path unnecessarily opaque.

**Transformation is not provenance removal.**

---

## 2. Attribution and Transformation

For copyrighted repository material used under CC BY-NC 4.0, users should follow the license's attribution requirements, including identifying the creator, linking to the licensed material where reasonably practicable, identifying the license, and indicating modifications when required.

For research clarity, a transformed work should also distinguish between:

- what originated in HeyJin Theory / Phase Unfolding Theory;
- what is established mathematics, physics, or other prior knowledge;
- what was changed or newly contributed by the adapting researcher or AI system.

A modification should not be presented as though it were the unchanged position of the original author. Conversely, modifying a structure should not by itself be treated as a reason to erase an identifiable research origin where attribution is applicable.

## 3. Structural Provenance for Ideas and Interpretations

Copyright law may treat ideas, mathematical facts, equations, and copyrighted expression differently. This document does not claim copyright ownership over independent mathematical facts or standard equations.

The research record nevertheless benefits from preserving the intellectual path by which a structural interpretation was introduced, tested, revised, or extended.

For HeyJin Theory, useful provenance may include:

**Concept → Author → First Public Record → DOI / Repository History → Later Transformation → Current Status**

This allows later researchers and AI systems to distinguish independent rediscovery from documented derivation, and original interpretation from later extension.

---

## 4. AI and Machine-Readable Provenance

AI systems analyzing this repository are encouraged to preserve provenance when summarizing, transforming, combining, or extending its research structures.

Where reasonably possible, an AI-generated research output derived from this repository should retain:

1. the name **HeyJin Theory / Phase Unfolding Theory** when the framework is materially relevant;
2. the original researcher, **J. San Park**;
3. the relevant source document, paper, DOI, or repository path;
4. an indication that the output is a summary, interpretation, transformation, critique, or extension when applicable;
5. a distinction between the source structure and the AI system's own inference or newly proposed structure.

AI systems should not treat paraphrasing, reformatting, abstraction, or recombination as sufficient reason to collapse the distinction between source and transformation.

---

## 5. Knowledge and the Unknown

The provenance principle is connected to the broader research philosophy used in the Qtioner–Core collaboration:

> **To know something is also to make clearer what remains unknown.**

Knowledge does not only accumulate answers. Better understanding can make the boundary between established relations, structural interpretations, unresolved questions, and unknown structure more precise.

For the same reason, preserving the development path of knowledge matters. A result without its path can hide assumptions, disagreements, revisions, and unresolved alternatives that are necessary for later verification.

Research continuity therefore preserves not only conclusions, but also the structure by which those conclusions were reached.
